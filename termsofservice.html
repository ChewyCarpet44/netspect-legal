2. Description of Service

NetSpect is a mobile application designed to facilitate pickup basketball matches between friend groups. The App provides:

  • Group creation and management — form groups and invite friends
  • Match coordination — schedule matches between groups
  • Credit system — a non-refundable, reusable accountability mechanism
  • GPS geofencing — detect player arrival at basketball courts
  • Location voting — group selection of match venues
  • Check-in verification — confirm attendance via GPS

The App is provided on an "AS-IS" basis without guarantees of availability, accuracy, or fitness for any particular purpose.

3. The Credit System

3.1 How Credits Work

Credits are:

  • Non-refundable — you do not receive a refund if you cancel a match after accepting it
  • Reusable — when you complete a match (detected via GPS check-in), the credit returns to your account
  • Required to compete — you must have an available credit to accept a match

3.2 Accepting a Match

When you accept a match invitation:

  1. One credit is reserved in your account (held until the match is completed or you no-show)
  2. The credit remains yours but is unavailable for other matches
  3. If the match is cancelled by the App, your credit is automatically returned

3.3 Completing a Match

When you check in at the court via GPS geofencing:

  1. Your location is verified server-side
  2. Upon verification, your reserved credit is returned to your account
  3. You can immediately use it for another match

3.4 If You Don't Check In

If you accept a match but fail to check in at the court, your reserved credit is not returned to you and remains unavailable for other matches. We do not currently apply any additional automated penalty (such as a lockout period) beyond this. We may introduce additional accountability measures in the future; if we do, this section will be updated to reflect them before they take effect.

4. Geofencing & Location Accuracy

4.1 How Geofencing Works

  • We use GPS technology to detect when you physically arrive at a basketball court
  • Geofencing is not real-time tracking — we do not track your movement continuously
  • We verify your location server-side before releasing your reserved credit
  • We compare your reported location to expected court coordinates

4.2 Accuracy Limitations

GPS is not 100% accurate. Limitations include:

  • GPS error margin of ±20–50 feet (weather, buildings, dense foliage)
  • Geofencing may not detect arrival if your device has weak GPS signal
  • Geofencing may not detect arrival if your location permission is delayed or disabled

We are not liable for:

  • GPS inaccuracy or device location errors
  • Missed check-ins due to poor signal
  • False positives (detection errors)
  • Device or user-caused location failures

4.3 Disputes & Appeals

If you believe your credit was incorrectly withheld after a match:

  1. Contact us within 7 days of the match with:
    • Match ID
    • Date and time of the match
    • Explanation of why you believe you were at the court
  2. We will review:
    • Your device's GPS logs
    • Court location records
    • Server-side verification data
  3. Resolution:
    • If we confirm you were likely at the court, we will restore your credit
    • If inconclusive, we may restore your credit at our discretion
    • If we determine the credit was correctly withheld, it stands
