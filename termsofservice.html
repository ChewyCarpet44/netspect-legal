2. Description of Service

NetSpect helps a group of people organise pickup basketball and records who actually turned up. The App provides: group creation and membership; calling a run at a court, now or on a future day; invite links that let other people join a run; a group chat; a map of courts with an indication of how busy each one has been; a list of people you have played with; and automatic check-in, which uses your device's location to confirm that you arrived at the court.

A run may be marked 3v3, 4v4 or 5v5. That is a display target only — it does not reserve a place, does not stop anyone else joining, and does not guarantee a game of that size or any game at all.

NetSpect coordinates people. It does not supply courts, referees, equipment, insurance, or supervision, and it is not a party to any game you arrange through it. The App is provided "AS-IS" without guarantees of availability, accuracy, or fitness for any particular purpose.

3. Invite Links & Group Membership

An invite link works for anyone who holds it. It is not tied to a phone number, an email address, or a person, and it can be forwarded on indefinitely by anyone who receives it. Whoever opens a valid link can join that run, and joining a run through a link also makes that person a member of the group that called it.

That is the intended behaviour, not a loophole — it is how a run gets filled by a friend of a friend. It also means you are responsible for who you send a link to and where it ends up. If you do not want someone in your group, do not send them a link and do not post one publicly.

A link can be turned off. The person who created a link can revoke it, and the person who called the run can revoke any link to it. Revoking stops future joins; it does not remove anyone who has already joined. Group membership is one group at a time — joining a new group through a link means leaving the one you are in.

5. Courts, Activity & Other Players

The App shows public courts on a map, with an indication of how busy each has been recently. That indication is aggregated: it is derived from how many distinct people checked in, never from who they were, and a court with fewer than three distinct people in the period is reported as having no activity rather than a small number. It is not a live feed of who is at a court, and it should not be read as one.

The App also shows you the names of people you have previously played with, so you can invite them again. They see your name on the same basis.

Courts listed in the App are real, public places we did not inspect and do not control. Availability, condition, safety, lighting, opening hours and legality of access are entirely outside our knowledge. A court appearing in NetSpect is not a recommendation and not permission to be there.

7. Geofencing & Location Accuracy

We use GPS to detect arrival at a court, and to confirm you are still there while a run is under way so that time at the court is measured rather than assumed. That confirmation happens opportunistically, when the App is already awake — it is not a continuous stream and we do not track your movement between courts. We verify your location server-side, comparing it to expected court coordinates, before releasing your reserved credit. GPS is not 100% accurate: expect an error margin of roughly ±20–50 feet depending on weather, buildings, or dense foliage, and geofencing may not detect arrival with a weak signal or delayed/disabled location permission. We are not liable for GPS inaccuracy, missed check-ins due to poor signal, false positives, or device/user-caused location failures.

If you believe your credit was incorrectly withheld after a match, contact us within 7 days with your match ID, the date/time, and why you believe you were at the court. We'll review your device's GPS logs, court location records, and server-side verification data. If we confirm you were likely there, we'll restore your credit; if inconclusive, we may restore it at our discretion; if we determine it was correctly withheld, it stands.
