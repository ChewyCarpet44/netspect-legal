1. Introduction

Crosspoint X ("Company," "we," "us," "our," or "Crosspoint X") operates the NetSpect mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Location Data

Geolocation Information:

  • We request "Always" location permission so that check-in can work even when the App isn't open, but we do not continuously track or stream your location
  • Instead, we use geofencing: your device monitors a small, specific boundary around a court only when you have an active match there, and notifies the App only when you cross that boundary — we do not receive or store a continuous trail of your movements
  • We verify your reported arrival server-side (comparing it against the court's known coordinates) before releasing your reserved credit
  • Location data is never sold or shared with third parties

What we do with location data:

  • Check-in verification: confirming you arrived at the court to release your reserved credit
  • Geographic filtering: showing available matches near your location

2.2 Account & User Data

When you sign up for NetSpect, we collect:

  • Anonymous Authentication ID (via Firebase Anonymous Auth — no email, password, or personal name required)
  • Group membership (which groups you've joined)
  • Credit history (a ledger of credits earned, reserved, and returned)
  • Match participation (which matches you've accepted and their outcomes)
  • Check-in records (timestamp and GPS coordinates of court arrivals)

2.3 Device Information

We automatically collect:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Unique device identifier (for app functionality only)
  • Basic app usage patterns

2.4 Firebase & Analytics

Your data is stored and processed through:

  • Firestore (Crosspoint X's primary database)
  • Firebase Authentication (anonymous sign-in only)
  • Firebase Cloud Functions (backend processing)

We do not use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or any Google Analytics products.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used for:

  1. App Functionality
    • Creating and managing your account
    • Processing group invitations and match participation
    • Managing the credit system
  2. Geofencing & Check-in
    • Detecting your arrival at courts via a geofence boundary crossing
    • Verifying that arrival server-side
    • Releasing your reserved credit upon verified arrival
    • If you accept a match and don't check in, your reserved credit is simply not released back to you — we do not currently apply any additional automated penalty beyond that
  3. Service Improvement
    • Analyzing match trends and game flow to improve the App
    • Debugging technical issues
  4. Safety & Legal Compliance
    • Resolving disputes (e.g., "I was there but wasn't detected")
    • Enforcing our Terms of Service
    • Complying with legal requests

4. Data Retention

4.1 While You Have an Account

  • Check-in records and credit history: Held for as long as your account exists, so the credit and accountability system can function
  • We do not retain a continuous location history — only the confirmation that a check-in occurred, tied to a specific match

4.2 If You Delete Your Account

Deleting your account through the App is a real, permanent deletion, not a deactivation:

  • Your profile, group membership, credit history, and pickup-game attendance records are permanently deleted from our database
  • Your account itself is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered
  • Some records that are shared with other users are not retroactively altered: group chat messages you sent remain visible to other group members under the name you had chosen at the time, and the outcome of a completed match involving other players remains part of the group's shared history. We don't scrub these after the fact, since doing so would change other members' own record of a shared event, not just yours.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT share your data with:

  • Advertisers
  • Data brokers
  • Social media platforms
  • Other third-party services

We DO share data only with:

  • Firebase Firestore (Google LLC) — for secure data storage and cloud functions
  • GitHub (Microsoft Corporation) — for encrypted code backups (your personal data is not on GitHub; only our source code is)

We may disclose your information if required by law (e.g., court order, legal subpoena).

6. Age Requirement

NetSpect is intended for users age 13 and older. By creating an account, you confirm that you are at least 13 years old. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 13 to use the App.

7. Your Privacy Rights

7.1 Access Your Data

You have the right to request:

  • A copy of all your personal data we hold
  • Information about how your data is used

7.2 Delete Your Account

You can delete your account at any time through the App settings (Settings → Delete Account). This permanently deletes your profile, group membership, credit history, and pickup-game attendance records — see Section 4.2 for exactly what is and isn't removed.

7.3 Opt Out of Location Tracking

You can disable location permissions in your device settings at any time. This will prevent check-ins from working, which means a reserved credit for a match you accept won't be released back to you.

7.4 California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data is collected
  • Know whether personal data is sold
  • Delete personal information
  • Opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell data)

To exercise these rights, contact us at: pointxcross@gmail.com

8. Data Security

How we protect your data:

  • All location data is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Firestore is protected by Google's enterprise-grade security
  • We verify location data server-side to prevent client-side manipulation
  • No personal identifiers (names, emails, phone numbers) are stored

What we cannot guarantee:

  • We cannot guarantee 100% security against unauthorized access
  • No security system is impenetrable
  • Use of the App is at your own risk

  • Your continued use of the App after changes means you accept the new policy
